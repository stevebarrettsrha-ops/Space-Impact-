SPACE IMPACT
Classic Side-Scrolling Shooter

Inspired by Nokia 3310 Classic
HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS

Arrow Keys - Move ship
SPACE - Fire weapon
P - Pause game

OBJECTIVE

Destroy enough ships per level to summon the boss.
Defeat the boss to advance.
Survive all 25 levels!

POWER-UPS

+ Health restore
Shield - Absorbs damage (50s)
Triple Shot - 3x bullets (30s)
Star - Invincible (5s)

ENEMIES

Fighters - Fast, low HP
Bombers - Tanky, shoots often
Scouts - Zigzag movement

BOSS & DANGERS

Three-Headed Hydra Boss (150 HP, 5000 pts) has 3 attacks:
Head Lunge - Stretches a head to bite you. Watch for the glow!
Charge - Rushes at you with a red aura, then retreats.
Snake Summon - Spawns shield-breaking snakes!
Speed intensifies - Game gets faster as you destroy more ships.
