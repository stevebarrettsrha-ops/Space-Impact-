HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS

Arrow Keys / WASD — fly
Space — fire (hold)
Shift — overdrive boost
P / Esc — pause

COINS 🪙

Enemies drop coins; bosses pay out big. Collect them mid-flight (your Magnet pulls them in), then spend them in the Hangar on permanent ship parts.

COMBO

Chain kills without getting hit to raise your combo multiplier — more score AND more coins. Taking a hit resets it.

POWER-UPS & ARMS

◆ weapon swap   ✛ repair
⬡ shield   ★ overcharge
6 weapons: Blaster · Spread · Laser · Cannon · Seeker · Wave

ENEMIES

Fighters rush, gunners stop & snipe with leading aim, divers swoop, swarms flock, kamikazes ram. They dodge — flank them.

BOSSES

Each sector ends with a multi-phase boss that enrages as its hull drops. Learn the tells, survive the barrage, claim the bounty.
